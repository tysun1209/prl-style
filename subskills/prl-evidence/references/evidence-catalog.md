## Implications and counterexamples

First figures can be apparatus or mechanism schematics as well as quantitative results. This defeats a mandatory result-first-panel rule. Color coexists with line/marker codes but is not always redundant. Several figures retain grids, dual axes, extensive legends, or dense multipanel comparisons; these are conditional design choices, not obligatory motifs. Serif/sans typography varies visually; no font-family frequency was measured. Numeric typography defaults must therefore be identified as local choices.

A published example is evidence of occurrence, not proof of optimal readability or current production compliance. These cards paraphrase limited passages and are not substitutes for reading a paper before citing its scientific claims.
